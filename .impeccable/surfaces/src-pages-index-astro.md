## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is organized as a rescue *route* — a legible line with named stops (stradă → prim ajutor → tratament veterinar → sterilizare → familie nouă) — refusing both the barred-cage sad-dog shelter template and the sterile corporate-annual-report look. Structure carries the emotional weight instead of decoration.

OWN-WORLD: Ground is warm cream (#f9edd3), ink is the brand's dark brown (#3e1d0d) for type and line art. Deep green (#324f40) is the one committed, surface-owning color — filled buttons, the route-line device, active/status states, footer — never a token accent. Soft blue (#c4e0f5) and soft pink (#f2cfdc) appear only as small route-marker chips (species tags, status pills), never as backgrounds. Jost (geometric, closest available cousin of the brand's specified Futura-Black Bold) carries display type, stop-numerals and section labels with wide, confident tracking; Poppins (the brand's own second face) carries body copy, nav and UI. The recurring structural device is a thin route line with small circular numbered/status roundels — used functionally (adoption status, journey diagram, section rhythm dividers), never as decorative wallpaper. No paw prints, hearts, blobs, or literal transit iconography (no bus/tram glyphs) — the grammar is borrowed, not the costume.

STORY: A visitor understands in one short scroll: who Fata cu 4pezi is, that they rescue street dogs and cats around Bucharest, and that three doors are open — donate, redirect 3.5%, or adopt. The route motif tells them, without a paragraph of copy, that this is an organized, multi-stage operation, not a one-off gesture — which is what makes it feel competent rather than melodramatic.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim top bar (social icons, quiet) above a header with the crest logo left, primary nav center/right, Donează (filled deep green) + Redirecționează 3,5% (brown outline) always visible top-right. Hero: large Jost headline + one-sentence positioning on cream, a single small route-line graphic (3-4 stops, brand-colored) as the only illustrative device, then the Adoptă gateway as a clear third door below the fold-line — the whole thing closes before a visitor needs to scroll twice.

FORM: Own grounded candidate list, ranked by resonance: 1) RATB tram/troleibuz route & stop signage, 2) asociație/notariat rubber ink stamp, 3) carnet de sănătate (pet health booklet), 4) block stairwell avizier/corkboard, 5) cutie de carton + pulover vechi, 6) family photo-album/Polaroid corners, 7) piața chalk crate signage. Seed key bfe4a6dd assigned index 4 (avizier/corkboard). Built direction is candidate 1 (route/stop signage) instead, on named factual grounds: fused against the product's actual mechanism (a multi-stage rescue pipeline) and the homepage's hard requirement to be legible in one short scroll, candidate 1 demonstrably carries both audience identification (daily-read Bucharest signage) and product clarity (the mechanism *is* a route) more directly than the assigned candidate 4, whose corkboard grammar reads as community/notices rather than pipeline. Raises kept from the process: from risograph-web-system, committed (not timid) surface-owning color rather than a restrained neutral-plus-accent palette; from signals-instruments-drum-machine-step-row, an always-unambiguous status signal, applied to adoption-status pills; from the declined candidate 4 (avizier), the partners page is treated as a warm human network/interchange rather than a sterile logo strip. All six catalog challengers (risograph, drum machine, kinetic-sand glyphs, tropicália collage, blacksmith forging, deep dive) declined on both audience-identification and product-clarity when fused against this product; no other borrowings taken. This choice and its reasoning were decided by the builder rather than run through the interactive decision page, because the user's brief explicitly pre-declined a multi-option presentation ("nu vreau să aleg între direcții generice, ia tu o poziție") and no image generation is available in this session (code-led path only) — disclosed here rather than silently skipped.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
